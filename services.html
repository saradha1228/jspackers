What We Offer

Transportation

We ensure that there comes no damage to goods, as the clients put their faith in us, and we respond to them to match their needs. Our fleet of well-maintained vehicles is available for local, outstation, and inter-city transportation of household goods, office items, and vehicles. We use proper strapping and padding to secure your belongings during transit.

Loading

Handling the consignment for relocation in India is not that easy, but we have made all such tasks look easy with our experience and expertise. Our trained professionals carefully load your goods using proper equipment and techniques to prevent damage during the loading process. We handle everything from small items to large furniture and appliances.

Unloading

Every possible attention is given to the loading and unloading of the materials at the destination of reach. At an affordable charge, we carefully unload all your belongings and place them in designated areas. Our team ensures that no damage occurs during the unloading process, making your relocation stress-free.

Adjusting

We place your belongings systematically and with utmost care. We also tidy the place after adjusting to make your stay comfortable. Our team helps arrange furniture and goods in your new home or office exactly as you want. We ensure that everything is in the right place before we leave.

Confirmation

We double-check the list before loading and after unloading to prevent things from getting lost. Our systematic inventory management ensures that every single item is accounted for at the origin and destination. You receive a complete list of all items for your records and peace of mind.

Tracking

We track your packages until it gets delivered to your doorstep and would be responsible for any delay on our way. Our real-time tracking system allows you to monitor the status of your shipment at every stage. You'll receive regular updates so you always know where your belongings are.

Delivery

We ensure you that the packages will be safely delivered to your doorstep. Our delivery service is prompt, reliable, and handled by experienced professionals. We guarantee timely delivery and take full responsibility for the safe arrival of all your goods.

Additional Services

Packing

Professional packing using high-quality materials — bubble wrap, corrugated boxes, foam padding — to keep your items safe during transit.

Storage

Secure, climate-controlled storage facilities available for short-term and long-term needs, perfect for staged moves or renovations.

Vehicle Relocation

Safe and insured transport of your car, bike, or other vehicles using enclosed carriers across India.

Commercial Moving

Specialised office and commercial relocation services with minimal downtime, ensuring your business operations are not disrupted.
